Contact

Email __CONTACT__ for anything: a problem with your account, a bug, a question about your data, or an idea. If you can still sign in, you can also send feedback from inside the app, with screenshots attached: Feedback is in the sidebar on the web and under More on the phone. Replies to feedback arrive at the email address on your account.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I use Beyou?

  • On the web, at __APP_URL__.
  • On Android, from Google Play.
  • On the iPhone, the app is in progress and not on the App Store yet.

It is the same account everywhere, so what you check on one device shows up on the others. Beyou is free.

How do I create an account and sign in?

With an email address and a password. Beyou sends a link to confirm the address, and you can sign in once you have opened it. If the email does not arrive, look in your spam folder, or ask for another one from the sign in screen.

You can also continue with Google, or with Apple on the iPhone app, and skip the password altogether. Either way, use the same option the next time you sign in.

I forgot my password

On the sign in screen, tap Forgot the password? and enter your email address. Beyou sends a link to choose a new password. It only works for a short time, so if it has expired, ask for a new one. If you created the account with Google or Apple there is no Beyou password to reset: sign in with that button again.

How do I delete my account and my data?

From inside the app, without having to ask anyone:

  • On the phone: More, then Config, then Delete my account in the danger zone at the bottom.
  • On the web: Config in the sidebar, then the same option.

Beyou emails you a six-digit code to confirm it is you. Once you confirm, the account and everything in it are erased immediately, and it cannot be undone. The same place has Download my data, if you want a copy first. If you cannot sign in any more, email __CONTACT__ from the address the account uses and ask for deletion. The privacy policy lists exactly what is erased.

What does the AI assistant do with what I write?

The assistant is optional. Nothing is sent to it unless you open it and write a message. When you do, your message and what it needs to answer go to an external AI provider, Mistral AI or Google Gemini. What you write in your diary is never sent. You can delete a conversation, or clear all of them, from the assistant itself.

A few other features use the same providers, and the daily briefing runs on its own once a day. Section 3 of the privacy policy describes each one and what it sends. If you would rather the daily briefing did not run for your account, write to __CONTACT__.

What happens to my data?

Beyou carries no ads and no trackers, and nothing about you is sold. The privacy policy says what is stored, who else sees it, how long it is kept and what your rights are.
